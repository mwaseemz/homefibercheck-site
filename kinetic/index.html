FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this Kinetic's website?

No. We are an independent Kinetic authorized agent. We are not Kinetic and we are not an internet service provider. We can check availability and place new Kinetic orders; the service itself is provided, installed, billed and supported by Kinetic.

Does it cost more to order through you?

No. You get the same Kinetic plans available at your address. We're paid by the provider when we set up a new customer, which doesn't change what you pay.

Why don't you list prices?

Kinetic's pricing and offers depend on your address and change over time. We'd rather give you the real monthly price for your home on the call than publish a number that turns out to be wrong.

Is Kinetic the same as Windstream?

Yes. Kinetic is the home internet brand of Windstream. We can check whether Kinetic Fiber has reached your address yet.

What if Kinetic Fiber isn't available at my address?

We'll tell you plainly. If fiber hasn't reached your street yet, we'll let you know rather than push you into a plan that doesn't fit.

I'm already a Kinetic customer and need help with my bill or service.

We can't help with existing accounts — we only set up new service. Please contact Kinetic directly at 1-800-347-1991 or through gokinetic.com for billing, technical support, moves or cancellations.

What do I need when I call?

Just your service address and a rough idea of how your household uses the internet. Most calls take about 10 minutes.
